Community privacy notice

Updated 10 November 2026

This dashboard helps the Morgan Minecraft community manage accounts, verified Minecraft identities and approved server operations. Contact the owner at morganluu75@gmail.com about your account or this notice.

Account and game data

Google sign-in supplies your account identifier, name, email and profile picture to Supabase Auth. Email accounts use Supabase Auth to handle credentials and verification. The dashboard stores a browser session so you can remain signed in. It does not request Gmail or Google Drive access.

Minecraft linking is separate: a short-lived challenge must be confirmed in game on an authenticated online-mode server. Verified UUIDs, usernames, membership decisions and assigned permissions support community access. Once the server integration is installed, it can collect playtime, deaths, mob kills, blocks mined, advancement counts and observed player activity. A first-recorded timestamp describes when collection first observed a player, not their first-ever visit.

Visibility and service providers

Your statistics are private by default. You can choose to share collected statistics with approved community members. Authorized operators can view server status, player lists, permitted console logs, command results and audit records according to their assigned permissions. Operational logs can include player names and game messages.

GitHub Pages hosts the frontend; Supabase provides authentication and cloud data; Google provides optional sign-in. These providers process requests under their own policies. Minecraft avatars are fetched from Crafatar using the displayed Minecraft UUID; no website credentials are sent with that image request. The application includes no advertising or third-party analytics scripts. Minecraft backup archive contents stay on the existing server.

Retention and archives

Terminal commands have a seven-day cloud retention target, audit events ninety days, backup metadata ninety days, and expired pairing records one additional day. Operational records are retained longer in the cloud whenever a verified local archive is unavailable. Uncertain commands remain until reconciled.

The local archive system is designed to encrypt and retain terminal commands for one year, audit history for two years, and backup metadata and periodic statistics/membership snapshots for one year. Worlds and actual Crafty backups follow their existing separate schedules. Memberships, permissions, player statistics and compact security summaries are preserved. Archive deployment and collection begin only after the owner installs and configures the Ubuntu integration.

Your choices

You can edit your display name and statistics-sharing preference in your account, and unlink a Minecraft identity through its private in-game confirmation. Ask the owner to inspect, correct or remove account information; protected operational audit records may remain under the retention policy. Do not enter passwords or other secrets into Minecraft commands or console messages.
